## Dashboard Overview

The main dashboard provides a high-level view of your marketing performance with key metrics and trends. Customize widgets to focus on the data that matters most to your business.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Conversions" icon="trending-up">
    Track total conversions, conversion value, and conversion rates across all channels.

    <Callout kind="info">Monitor conversion velocity to identify peak performance periods.</Callout>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Attribution" icon="pie-chart">
    See how credit is distributed across your marketing touchpoints and channels.

    <Expandable title="Attribution Breakdown" default-open="false">

    </Expandable>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ROI Analysis" icon="dollar-sign">

  </Tab>

## Custom Reporting

Create detailed reports tailored to your specific business needs. Share insights with team members or automate report delivery.

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Report Builder" default-open="true">
    Use our drag-and-drop report builder to create custom visualizations and data tables.

    <CodeGroup tabs="Dashboard,API">
      ```javascript
// Create custom report
const report = {
  name: 'Monthly Performance',
  metrics: ['conversions', 'revenue', 'roas'],
  dimensions: ['channel', 'campaign'],
  filters: { dateRange: 'last_30_days' },
  schedule: 'monthly'
};
await anytrack.createReport(report);
      ```
      ```javascript
// API access
const reports = await anytrack.getReports({
  type: 'custom',
  limit: 10
});
      ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Automated Reports" default-open="false">
    Schedule reports to be generated and delivered automatically via email or API webhook.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Team Sharing" default-open="false">
    Share reports with team members and set appropriate permission levels for data access.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

| Report Type | Frequency | Recipients | Use Case |
|-------------|-----------|------------|----------|
| Daily Summary | Daily | Marketing Team | Quick performance check |
| Weekly Deep Dive | Weekly | Management | Strategic planning |
| Monthly ROI | Monthly | Executives | Business review |
| Campaign Specific | Ad-hoc | Campaign Managers | Campaign optimization |

<Callout kind="info">Reports can be exported in multiple formats including PDF, CSV, and Excel.</Callout>

## Attribution Analysis

Dive deep into how your marketing channels work together to drive conversions. Understand the customer journey and optimize your marketing mix.

<Expandable title="Path Analysis" default-open="false">
  Visualize customer journeys from first touch to conversion. Identify common paths and optimization opportunities.

  ```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Organic Search] --> B[Email Newsletter]
    B --> C[Paid Social]
    C --> D[Conversion]
    A --> E[Direct Visit]
    E --> D
  ```
</Expandable>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Attribution Model" icon="target">Choose from first-touch, last-touch, or data-driven models.</Step>
  <Step title="Analyze Channel Performance" icon="bar-chart">Compare ROI across different marketing channels.</Step>
  <Step title="Identify Optimization Opportunities" icon="lightbulb">Find underperforming channels and high-potential areas.</Step>
  <Step title="Test Changes" icon="flask">Run A/B tests on attribution models or channel allocations.</Step>
  <Step title="Implement Improvements" icon="check">Apply insights to optimize your marketing strategy.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="danger">Remember that different attribution models can significantly impact ROI calculations. Choose the model that best reflects your customer journey.</Callout>
